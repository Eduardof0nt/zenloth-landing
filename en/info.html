Web app

Zenloth is a web app. You don't need to download anything from the App Store or Google Play. You sign in from any browser at zenloth.tech with your account and your data always in sync.

  • On any device: phone, tablet or computer, same experience.
  • Like a native app: add it to your home screen (PWA) and it opens full-screen.
  • No manual updates: you always have the latest version, instantly.

Why web and not the stores? To move fast, reach everyone equally, and skip store fees. Your money deserves focus, not friction.

Security

Your financial information is among the most sensitive there is. We treat it that way, from day one.

  • We encrypt your data at rest and in transit.
  • Data hosted in Costa Rica, under local regulation.
  • We never sell your data. Our business is your subscription, not your information.
  • You're in control: export or delete all your information anytime.

If you find something we should review, write to nacho@zenloth.tech. We take it seriously.

About us

Zenloth was born in Costa Rica with a simple idea: personal finance shouldn't cause anxiety. We took the calm of the rainforest and turned it into a tool that shows you where you are, where you're going, and your next step.

Zenny, our sloth guide, is the reminder that moving slowly is still moving forward. No guilt, no scary numbers: clarity so you make better decisions at your own pace.

What we believe

  • Calm over alarm. We warn you before, not after.
  • Built for Costa Rica: colones and dollars, together and tidy.
  • Your data is yours. Period.
